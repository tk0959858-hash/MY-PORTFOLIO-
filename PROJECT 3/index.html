Popular Varieties

Sindhri

Known for its large size, sweet flavor, and soft texture. Often called the honey mango.

Chaunsa

Exceptionally sweet with a unique fragrance. A favorite for juice and direct consumption.

Anwar Ratol

Small in size but packs an incredibly sweet and aromatic punch.

Langra

Stays green even when ripe. Famous for its distinct, slightly fibrous texture and strong flavor.
